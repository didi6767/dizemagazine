About DIZÉ

For faces with
something to say.

DIZÉ is an independent models magazine shaped in Kuala Lumpur, celebrating the people who turn an image into a story.

Our point of view

A model is never just the face in the frame.

We make space for the personality, ambition and point of view behind every photograph. DIZÉ profiles the models whose presence gives an image its energy.

Our work is intimate, clear-eyed and generous. It begins with a portrait and looks beyond it.
